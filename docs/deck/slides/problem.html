THE PROBLEM

Fewest sensors, least robust design

Goal: every substation observable. We solved it exactly, then priced in a failed PMU (IEEE 14, $110k per PMU).

Exact optimum on the larger TVA grids: IEEE 30 needs 10, 57 needs 17, 118 needs 32, 300 needs 87, each solved in under 40 ms.

The point of this slide: the headline number, four PMUs, is not the whole answer. Redundancy costs money and the planner chooses where to stand. Costs use the DOE 2014 range of $40k to $180k per installed PMU, mid-case $110k. Source: results/pareto.json and results/baseline.json.
